# Publication evidence audit

Version 1.0.0 · 16 September 2026

## Sources inspected

- Supplied design prompt and commercial recommendations: design and offer guidance, not independent verification of commercial results.
- Escape Pod: `content/portfolio.json`, `STRATEGY-LOCK.md`, `SOURCE-AUDIT.md`, ProposalDesk source and CV extraction.

## Public claims

- Twenty years refers to the professional career beginning in 2006, including teaching and overlapping appointments, not twenty years in every specialty.
- Ten countries is the regional EWS assignment scope, not a claim of on-the-ground delivery in ten countries.
- The green-transition research scope is 400 firms, 120 interviews, eight focus groups; no emissions or policy-adoption impact is claimed.
- Jolshiri’s USD 1,414 million capital scope and TVET’s USD 44.108 million estimated budget appear in the supplied September CV extraction (lines 938 and 632 respectively). Neither is represented as money managed or raised.
- The website includes 28 detailed assignments from the supplied structured record. The workbook contains 46 distinct documented records; the website does not imply that its index is the full career total.
- Email, business phone and WhatsApp destination are supplied in the portfolio JSON, without independent account ownership verification.

## Conflicts handled without inventing resolutions

The JSON cites a September 2026 CV, while the workbook often cites a May 2026 CV. Education project dates conflict across these sources. EWS ranges end in August or December 2026; only 2025–2026 is published, with planned-output qualification. Education project rows use a portfolio label. Payra, HALOW, RESA and tobacco-taxation date variants are omitted. CEM and green trade are shown at year granularity. Historical degree completion years are omitted from public pages and the downloadable profile.

## Commercial limits

No invented testimonial, client endorsement, income, tender-win rate or product usage count. ProposalDesk is offered as a direct professional service; no software account or checkout is simulated. Clinic and toolkit status labels describe proposed/development stages. Institutional names are plain text and do not imply endorsement. Portrait, LinkedIn and historical research publications are not fabricated.
